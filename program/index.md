    </div>
    <div class="section-body">
      <p>The AI/CRV 2026 program brings together Monday workshops, the Canadian AI Conference, the Conference on Robots &amp; Vision, the Responsible AI track, the Industry Track, student programming, posters, and social events.</p>
      <p>This schedule is tentative and subject to change. For the most detailed track information, see the <a href="https://www.caiac.ca/en/conferences/canadianai-2026/program" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Canadian AI program</a>, the <a href="https://www.computerrobotvision.org/program.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener">CRV program</a>, and the <a href="https://www.caiac.ca/en/conferences/canadianai-2026/responsible-ai" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Responsible AI program</a>.</p>
